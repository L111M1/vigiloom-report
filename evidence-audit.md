# Pregabalin evidence review — 1 November 2026

This is a subsequent researcher audit of the generated report, not a replacement model assessment. The published HTML is an unchanged copy of `runs/pregabalin_four_v1/report.html`. All 24 investigations reached completed status; completion is not evidence of causal validity or literature-search completeness.

## Important coverage corrections

The report's absence-of-record conclusions are limited by its captured sources. A historical Canadian LYRICA monograph (11 May 2020, pp. 26–28) lists hypothyroidism, tooth caries, iron-deficiency anemia and osteoporosis among treatment-emergent events. It explicitly says these events were not necessarily caused by LYRICA. These outcomes cannot be described as previously unrecorded.

- [Canadian LYRICA monograph](https://pdf.hres.ca/dpd_pm/00056333.PDF)
- [EMA Pregabalin Accord product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/pregabalin-accord-epar-product-information_en.pdf) lists gastroesophageal reflux disease.
- [2025 population-based osteoporosis study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2025.1522937) reports a pregabalin association; its observational design does not establish drug causation.
- [Pregabalin-associated acute interstitial nephritis case](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jsdt/50/5/50_301/_article/-char/en) means acute renal injury cannot be called unreported. It does not establish incident chronic kidney disease.
- [PMDA clinical-trial safety tables](https://www.pmda.go.jp/drugs/2010/P201000071/671450000_22200AMX00297_K105_1.pdf) contain IBS events, without establishing causation.

## Specific propositions still lacking direct support in the material reviewed

These are remaining evidence gaps, not confirmed new adverse reactions or proof that no publication exists:

- Pregabalin initiating or aggravating IBS specifically during tapering/withdrawal. Existing IBS efficacy research and adverse-event counts answer different questions.
- Pregabalin causing new, persistent chronic kidney disease, distinguished from acute injury, pre-existing impairment and accumulation.
- Pregabalin causing tongue fissuring, distinguished from already-recorded tongue swelling and other tongue events.
- A specific 48-hour sleep rhythm or delayed circadian phase attributable to pregabalin, distinguished from recorded insomnia/withdrawal insomnia.
- Pregabalin-induced mucus overproduction leading to recurrent choking and aspiration pneumonitis; airway warnings alone do not demonstrate this chain.
- Reduced gastric acid mediating clinically meaningful B12 deficiency during pregabalin treatment; serum B12 observations do not demonstrate that mechanism.
- Bone-density recovery after gabapentinoid discontinuation; existing bone studies do not directly validate the community author's reversal narrative.

For other signals, a label entry or published association still cannot establish causation in an individual forum account. Use candidate-level sources and post context together.
